FAQ

The things people usually ask.

What do I need to send before you start?

A short description of the business or project, the pages or screens you need, required features, any logo/photos/text you already have, examples of styles you like, and your target budget.

Do you make the site work on phones too?

Yes. Responsive behavior is part of the normal build, not an extra visual pass at the end.

Can you redesign an existing website or app?

Yes. If the current project already has useful content or functionality, the redesign can preserve what makes sense and focus on structure, usability, responsiveness, and visual quality.

Are domain and hosting costs included?

Not automatically. I can help prepare or connect the project, but paid hosting, domains, APIs, licenses, and similar third-party fees are separate unless the quote says otherwise.

Will I get the source code?

Source handoff is available and can be included in the final scope so you’re not locked into a mystery builder or account.

What if I ask for more features after we start?

Small agreed revisions are handled inside the project. New pages, screens, or features that noticeably change the scope can change the final quote, and I’ll tell you before adding them.
