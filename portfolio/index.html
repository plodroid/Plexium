FAQ

Stuff people usually ask.

Are you available?

Yes. We’re currently available for new website and app work.

Are the designs custom?

Yes. The goal is to build around the business and its content instead of forcing every client into the same template.

Why are these prices lower than some online agencies?

Because the pricing is made for a different market and scope. A basic website online can easily start around $40 or more, while local projects may need a more practical starting point.

Do you offer long-term support?

Yes. Yearly support is available, and a permanent support option can be added for an extra 10,000 DZD depending on scope.
