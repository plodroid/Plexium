FAQ

Stuff people usually ask.

How long does a website take?

Simple websites usually take 1–2 days. Bigger sites are often 2–3 days, while heavier work can take around a week.

How long does an app take?

Simple apps are usually 2–3 days. Bigger builds are often 6–10 days, and very involved apps can reach 1–2 weeks.

Is the design custom?

Yes. The point is to fit the business instead of dropping every client into the same template.
