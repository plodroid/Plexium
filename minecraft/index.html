BoxPvP

The main story

BoxPvP is a progression PvP game. You begin with basic gear, mine resources in the arena, convert them into character upgrade blocks, then evolve your equipment through 20 SpongeBob-themed character sets.

1
Build your starter kitUse the starter shop for your first armor, tools, food and utility.
2
Mine your current tierEvery character level has a specific mine material.
3
Convert 64 → 164 mine blocks become one custom Character Block for that tier.
4
Upgrade and move forwardYour previous character gear is required to craft the next set.

Progression

All 20 character levels

Each level has its own mine material. Convert 64 of it into one Character Block, then combine Character Blocks with your previous gear.

LevelCharacterMine materialSword / Pickaxe / AxeEach armor piece
Upgrade costs grow in groups of three levels: early gear uses 6/12 Character Blocks, while endgame reaches 42/84.
Economy

Shop & resource flow

Items and money work together. Common resources can be traded into gold currency, while higher-value Gold Blocks and Diamond Blocks unlock stronger utility.

Resource exchangeEmeralds, diamonds, coal, iron and sponge can be traded for gold currency.
Diamond Blocks64 Gold Blocks can be exchanged for 1 Diamond Block.
UtilityFood, rockets, bows, arrows, ender pearls, water buckets, apples and shulker boxes are sold here.
EnchantingBuy enchanted books and XP bottles to improve gear.

Crates

Keys & crate rewards

PlexiumMC has six key tiers: Party, Vote, VIP, MVP, Plex and Plexium. Use /crates to view keys and crate rewards.

Party crate rolls can upgrade into stronger keys: 65% Vote, 19% VIP, 10% MVP, 5% Plex and 1% Plexium.

Plexium Keys are the rarest Party-key upgrade at only 1%.

Mining utility

Mine TNT

Mine TNT is a progression mining tool sold in four tiers. It clears mine blocks around the blast rather than acting like ordinary griefing TNT.

Trading

Auction House

Use /ah to browse player listings. Hold an item and use /ah sell <price> to list it. Sold auctions pay directly into your BoxPvP balance.

Island Wars

Knock them into the void.

Island Wars is a knockback progression game. Fight on islands, earn money by dealing damage, throw opponents into the void for bonus cash, then spend that money on stronger swords.

Progression

How to progress

1
Start with Rookie TapbladeTier 1 is free.
2
Fight for moneyEvery point of damage pays $1.
3
Secure void killsEarn a $10 bonus when a qualifying target falls within 15 seconds.
4
Unlock stronger swordsUse /shop and climb all 45 tiers to Titan Mace.

Combat

Passives & right-click abilities

Every sword has normal knockback, a chance-based passive, and a separate right-click active ability with a cooldown. Higher tiers add pulls, pushes, dashes, freezes, swaps, domains, meteors, clones and other control tools.

The best sword is not only raw knockback. Learn your passive proc and active cooldown.
